# Project 3: Build a Go-to-Market Plan for Your Side Hustle

## Business

### Founder-Led Demand Content System

Help B2B SaaS founders turn their expertise, customer conversations, product insight, and company knowledge into LinkedIn + newsletter content that helps buyers understand the problem, trust the company, and enter sales conversations better informed.

## Positioning

Not:

> Ghostwriting for founders.

Instead:

> I turn what B2B founders already know into a repeatable content system that supports buyer education, category authority, and sales conversations.

## Core problem

Founders already possess useful market knowledge, but much of it stays trapped in:

- Customer calls
- Sales conversations
- Product meetings
- Internal documents
- Slack
- The founder's head

The market only sees a small percentage of it.

## Mechanism

```text
Founder expertise
      ↓
Customer / sales insight
      ↓
Research
      ↓
Founder POV
      ↓
LinkedIn + newsletter
      ↓
Buyer education
      ↓
Better-informed conversations
```

AI supports research, extraction, organization, ideation, repurposing, and workflow management.

Human judgment remains responsible for POV, positioning, stories, editing, account context, and final writing.

## ICP

- B2B SaaS
- Roughly 20–150 employees
- Seed to Series B preferred
- U.S. market or active U.S. expansion
- Complex product requiring education
- Founder still involved in sales / positioning / market education
- Growth, hiring, funding, launch, or expansion trigger
- Founder content has room to improve

## Trigger signals

Prioritize companies with:

1. New-market expansion
2. New GTM or sales leadership
3. Recent funding
4. Growing headcount
5. Product launch or repositioning
6. Founder already posting inconsistently
7. Company content stronger than founder content
8. Repeated buyer-education problem
9. Sales team repeatedly explaining the same category problem

## Entry offer

### 30-Day Founder Demand Pilot

Deliverables:

- Founder positioning interview
- Message / content map
- 8 LinkedIn posts
- 1 newsletter or long-form email
- Customer / sales insight mining
- Performance and learning review

$2,000 for 30 days.

This is a pricing test, not a claim about market benchmark pricing.

## Landing page role

The landing page is not expected to acquire traffic by itself. Its purpose is to validate the offer when a researched prospect clicks after outreach.

It should answer:

1. Who is this for?
2. What problem does it solve?
3. What outcome does it support?
4. What is different about the approach?
5. What does the pilot include?
6. Why trust the operator?
7. What should the prospect do next?
