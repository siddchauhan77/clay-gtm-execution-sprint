# Project 2: Build a 90-Second Account Brief

## Selected account

# Wooqer

Wooqer was selected because it combined a strong product, clear enterprise proof, active North America expansion, founder involvement, and a plausible founder-led demand opportunity.

## Company snapshot

Wooqer is a frontline operations platform for multi-location businesses, especially retail and restaurant chains.

Its product helps teams manage areas such as:

- Store audits
- Opening / closing checklists
- Visual merchandising compliance
- Frontline communication
- Training and compliance
- Corrective actions
- Operational analytics

The underlying problem is the execution gap between what headquarters expects and what happens across distributed locations.

```text
HQ defines standard
       ↓
Hundreds of locations
       ↓
Execution varies
       ↓
Poor visibility
       ↓
Customer experience + compliance suffer
```

## Why now

The central trigger is North America expansion.

The company moved from general international growth to an explicit North America motion, including new sales leadership and U.S.-specific market positioning.

This creates a useful GTM question:

> How does Wooqer transfer strong global credibility into U.S. awareness, trust, and pipeline?

## 90-second brief

Wooqer is a frontline operations platform for multi-location businesses, particularly retail and restaurant chains. It helps companies standardize store audits, checklists, visual merchandising, compliance, frontline communication, and operational reporting across distributed locations.

The timing is interesting because Wooqer has moved into an active North America expansion. Founder and CEO Vishal Purohit publicly announced the North America push, while the company added sales leadership focused on building the regional motion.

Wooqer already has strong global proof. The GTM challenge appears to be translating that credibility into U.S. awareness, trust, and pipeline.

My hypothesis is that founder-led content could support this expansion by turning Wooqer's accumulated operational knowledge, customer stories, and U.S. market learning into useful category education for retail operations leaders.

Instead of generic product promotion, I would test content around the execution gap inside multi-location businesses, lessons from global retail operators, common operational failures, and what Wooqer is learning while building its North American business.

The goal would be to connect the founder voice with the new U.S. sales motion and create more informed conversations with the operators Wooqer wants to reach.
