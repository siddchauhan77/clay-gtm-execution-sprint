## Editorial priority and distribution

1. **Publish carousel 1 to reach career switchers and GTME operators.** Reply to relevant hiring posts with the focused proof, not the broad carousel link.
2. **Publish carousel 2 to demonstrate technical judgment.** Link the repository, run logs and source register in your caption or first comment.
3. **Record one 60-second screen walkthrough** of the existing simulator. Show a first run, safe rerun and conflicted record. No need for a fourth project to create this content.

## Editorial accuracy notes

- Do not claim a certification or course caused Fermin's hire.
- Do not equate a self-reported transition with a population-wide hiring probability.
- Attribute Grok/Apollo performance results to the operator/vendor and omit causal certainty.
- Keep outreach and revenue labeled pending.
- Never present the local CRM simulator as a live integration.
