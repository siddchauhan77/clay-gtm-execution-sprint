The problem

Your best material rarely starts as a post.

It starts as a sharp answer on a sales call, a customer story, a product decision, or a belief you repeat every week. Most of it disappears before the market sees it.

01

Expertise stays private.

Good thinking lives in calls, docs, Slack, and the founder's head.

02

Company content sounds like company content.

Product pages explain features. Buyers still need a point of view and context.

03

Publishing becomes another founder task.

Content gets pushed behind hiring, product, sales, fundraising, and customers.

The system

Extract. Sharpen. Publish. Learn.

Fit

Useful when content supports a real business motion.

Good fit

  • B2B SaaS with a complex product
  • Founder still shapes sales, positioning, or category education
  • Launching, hiring, raising, repositioning, or entering a market
  • Strong customer knowledge but inconsistent founder publishing
  • You want content connected to buyer questions and GTM priorities

Not the fit

  • You only want daily volume
  • You want a fake personality or manufactured hot takes
  • You already run a mature founder-led content machine
  • You want content disconnected from customers, product, or sales
  • You measure success only by impressions
