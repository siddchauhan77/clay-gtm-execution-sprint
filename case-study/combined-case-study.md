# Combined Case Study: From Clay Research to GTM Execution

## Challenge

Build a real GTM campaign using Handshake AI Skills Studio / Clay projects, without turning the work into another learning or certification exercise.

Every project had to produce something that was public, usable, measurable, or connected to a real human.

## System

```text
Business / Offer
      ↓
ICP
      ↓

      ↓

      ↓

      ↓

      ↓

      ↓
Messaging
      ↓
Landing page
      ↓
Outreach
      ↓
Results
      ↓
Lessons
```

## Project 1: Target selection

Clay was used to source and enrich 25 accounts. The list was narrowed to eight for deeper human research and five final priority accounts.

The important insight was that data enrichment alone was insufficient. Human research changed the ranking because companies such as Aligned and CloudEagle.ai looked attractive based on company quality but had already solved much of the founder-content problem.

## Project 2: Account intelligence

Wooqer became the primary account because of active North America expansion, strong global proof, founder involvement, and a product that requires significant buyer education.

The account brief transformed raw company information into a specific hypothesis:

> Use the founder's global operating knowledge, customer insight, and U.S. market learning as a category-education layer supporting the new North America sales motion.

## Project 3: GTM plan

The side-hustle offer became a 30-day Founder Demand Pilot. The plan defined the ICP, triggers, positioning, mechanism, channels, funnel, pricing test, campaign metrics, and portfolio narrative.

## Landing page

A one-page campaign site was built to validate the offer after outbound outreach. The page deliberately uses process evidence rather than fabricated client results.
